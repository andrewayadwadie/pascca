Both amendments were approved by the client on 2026-08-19 and are now law, not proposals.

| # | Article | What changed | Landed in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **16 [NN]** | Light warm base (`--bg:#FBF7F0`); the v2.0.0 dark values scoped to `[data-surface="dark"]`. Gold `#D4AF37`, Zodiak and Plus Jakarta Sans unchanged. No token name removed. | `packages/config/tokens.css`, Art. 16 block |
| 2 | **19 [NN]** | Motion budget 7 → 10 items. A lazy-loaded motion library is permitted for items 8–10, and a poster-first video hero is item 10, under mandatory video rules. | Art. 19 |
| 3 | **17 [NN]** | Glass nav de-literalised from `rgba(10,10,10,.95)` to `--bg-95`, so it follows whichever treatment it sits in. | Art. 17 |

**Two things the palette flip forced, both verified rather than assumed:**

- The light ink scale is **not** the dark scale re-tinted. Alphas were solved against
  `--surface-3` (#EAE0D1, the darkest light surface) so every text token clears AA 4.5:1.
  `--w50` and `--w40` converge at the AA floor because on a light surface four legible
  alpha steps below full ink do not exist; hierarchy below `--w60` is carried by size,
  weight and letter-spacing, which `globals.css` already did.
- `--w40` under the **dark** treatment was raised from `.4` to `.55`. At `.4` it gave
  3.77:1 on 11px uppercase labels — a **pre-existing v2.0.0 accessibility defect**, not a
  v3.0.0 regression. Article 28 [NN] requires the floor, so it was fixed rather than
  carried forward.

`scripts/check-token-contrast.mjs` now recomputes every ink/surface pair in both
treatments and runs in CI beside the hex-literal check, so a future token edit cannot
quietly drop text below 4.5:1.

**Still open, deliberately:** Article 17 pins the floating plate's shadow to
`rgba(0,0,0,.5)` by literal value. On cream that reads heavier than it did on black.
Softening it needs its own Article 17 amendment and has not been made.

## Design Context

### Users

Two audiences on the same eight routes, both mostly on a phone:

- **Cairo locals who already know Pascca** — deciding tonight. Price, distance, open-or-not,
  then call or book. Speed is the whole experience.
- **Discovery visitors from Instagram, Google, or a delivery app** — 26.3K people follow the
  Instagram account, and the food photography is what brought them. They arrive already looking
  at pictures. The site's job is to not be a downgrade from the feed.

Staff use `apps/admin`: non-technical operators editing menu, prices, images, and page copy, and
handling reservations. `ADMIN` has everything; `MODERATOR` is scoped to
`reservation:read/create/update/delete` + `message:read/update` — already seeded, already
enforced through `requirePermission`, never through a role check in a component.

### Brand Personality

**Warm · Generous · Unpretentious.**

Pascca is the place you go back to every week, not the place you save up for. Two facts anchor
this and neither may be softened: **EGP 200–400 per person**, and **prices are visible
everywhere a dish appears**. The voice is plain, warm, and quietly funny. Never luxury-formal,
never scarce, never "inquire for pricing."

The emotional goal is **appetite plus ease** — the visitor should feel hungry and feel that
booking is a thirty-second job.

What the Instagram account reveals that the current build does not: Pascca is **two restaurants
in one day**. Heavy breakfast and brunch content — eggs, pancakes, pastries, coffee — alongside
the pizza and pasta. The site currently tells only the dinner half of the story.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Direction: light, warm, food-first — with dark kept as a deliberate accent, not a default.**

- **Surfaces:** cream and warm off-white as the base. Photography sits on light ground the way
  it does in the feed. Dark (`#0A0A0A` / `#141414`) is retained and used *on purpose* — footer,
  evening/dinner sections, the reservation panel — so the existing dark tokens keep earning
  their place instead of being deleted.
- **Accent:** gold `#D4AF37` survives as the only accent, unchanged. On light ground it needs a
  darker companion for any text use; gold remains **banned for body copy** in both worlds — an
  accessibility rule, not a style one.
- **Typography:** unchanged. **Zodiak** (self-hosted, already in `apps/web/public/fonts`) for
  display, **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body. Zodiak is a warm serif and reads better on cream than
  it does on black — this direction costs nothing typographically. No font CDN.
- **Photography is the design.** Real dish and interior photos, exported by the client from Meta
  and processed through R2 → WebP/AVIF at three sizes with blurHash. Until they land, every slot
  renders a **designed placeholder** — never stock, never AI-generated food, never an empty box.
- **Motion:** purposeful, appetite-serving. Reveal on scroll, staggered grids, hover on dish
  cards, filter transitions, the 3D floating plate. Everything disabled under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`. Video where it earns its place, poster-first.
- **Bilingual by construction:** logical CSS properties only, so Arabic is a flag flip, not a
  rebuild.

**Anti-references** — what this must not become:

- A dark "fine dining" site that implies a price the restaurant does not charge. That is a
  direct Article 2 violation, and it is the failure mode the current build is closest to.
- A template restaurant site: stock pasta photo, full-bleed hero with centered serif, three
  identical feature cards, a menu locked in a PDF.
- Scroll-jacked, parallaxed, or cursor-trailed. Motion that makes a hungry person wait is a
  broken button.
- A site that hides prices, hides hours, or makes finding the nearer branch take more than one
  tap.

**Real proof to use, never to invent:** 4.1★ from 417 Google reviews · #120 of 8,156 Cairo
restaurants on Restaurant Guru · 26.3K Instagram followers · three consented guest testimonials ·
outdoor seating, wheelchair accessible, takeaway, delivery via talabat and elmenus. Anything not
on that list does not go on the page.

### Design Principles

1. **The photograph is the argument.** Pascca is sold by how the food looks. Layout exists to
   frame photography, not to compete with it. A section with no real image is a section with a
   deliberately designed absence — never a stock stand-in.
2. **Price is never hidden.** Every dish shows its price, everywhere it appears. This is
   positioning, not data completeness, and it is non-negotiable.
3. **Two restaurants, one day.** Breakfast and dinner are both first-class. The design has room
   for a bright morning and a warm evening, which is exactly why the palette needs both a light
   base and a dark treatment.
4. **Booking is thirty seconds.** From any page, on a phone, one hand. Party ≤6 confirms
   instantly; >6 says plainly that someone will call. No account, no funnel, no upsell.
5. **Motion serves appetite, never ego.** Every animation either reveals food, confirms an
   action, or gets out of the way. If it delays a tap or costs a Lighthouse point without
   earning it, it is cut.

**The API collection for mobile is not a separate build.** Every endpoint from 005 onward is
under `/api/v1`, enveloped, documented in OpenAPI 3.1 generated from Zod, with types published to
`packages/types`. The Flutter app gets a Postman collection tag per Article 33 and calls the
identical surface the website calls. There is no second backend.

## Assets still owed by the client

Blocking work, in order of impact:

1. **Photography** — dish and interior, exported from Meta Business Suite or original files from
   the photographer. Originals beat compressed Instagram copies significantly.
2. **Logo / wordmark** — a real file. No substitute mark gets drawn and shipped.
3. **Reels / video** — if the video-hero direction stands.
4. **Press / awards specifics** — the client reports real ones; nothing has been supplied, so
   nothing goes on the page.

Instagram and Facebook cannot be scraped for these — login walls, tokenised CDN URLs, and
platform terms all block it. The files have to be handed over directly.
