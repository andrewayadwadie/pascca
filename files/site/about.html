Our story

Freshly baked, every day

"Some come to Pascca for the food, others come for emotional support. We serve both."

That line started as a joke on our Instagram and became the most accurate description of the place we've ever written. People come here for a Friday breakfast that runs three hours, for a first date, for a birthday nobody wants to leave. The food has to be good enough to hold them there — so the dough is proved properly, the sauce is made every morning, and produce arrives daily rather than weekly.

Nothing about that changed when we opened Heliopolis. It just gave us a bigger room and a longer morning.
